My first ever Godot game that I'm building entirely from scratch~

This game is inspired by Welcome To The Game 3 

You are someone who has in need of cash and decided to take up freelance hacking. A mysterious person decided to be your first decipherer and mentor. Earn reputation and money and climb your way to becoming the greatest hacker ever

Every night, you are needed to pay 100 for rent.

In total, there are 5 quests in the game and the quests changes every day.

This game was an idea I had in my head for a while and I thought since I just started learning Godot, this would be a great time to start this project.
